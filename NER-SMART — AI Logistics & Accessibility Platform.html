GEOGRAPHICAL & LOGISTICAL GROUND TRUTH

Why Northeast India Needs Intelligent Logistics

Covering 262,000 sq km connected to mainland India solely by the 22-km wide Siliguri Corridor, the Northeast faces unique geological and climatic disruptions where standard algorithms fail.

1. Mountain Terrain & Ghat Passes

Elevation ranges from 25m in Barak Valley to 3,500m+ at Sela Pass. Sharp hairpins and high slope resistance heavily impact heavy multi-axle freight.

2. Record Monsoon Precipitation

Meghalaya’s southern face receives over 11,000 mm of annual rainfall. Flash torrents wash across roads, turning highways into mud channels within 30 minutes.

3. Landslides & Subsidence

Sonapur Tunnel (NH-6) and Paglapahar (NH-29) suffer frequent shear slope failures, trapping freight convoys carrying medical supplies and food grains.

4. Riverine Floods & Embankments

The Brahmaputra and Barak river networks inundate national highways across Nagaon, Kaziranga, and Cachar during June–September.

5. Fragile Bridges & Load Limits

Over 200 single-lane or wooden-deck Bailey bridges have 16–25 ton ceilings. Heavy multi-axle trailers routed blindly risk structural collapse.

6. Single-Lifeline Arteries

States like Tripura, Mizoram, and Barak Valley rely almost solely on NH-6. A single blockage cuts off 8 million citizens unless alternate bypasses exist.
